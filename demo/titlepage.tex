\begin{articleabstract}
This paper treats a typeset page as an instrument for reading.
It shows how lanepaper v3 uses a restrained hierarchy, regular rhythm, and
small typographic corrections to let structure carry an argument.
The examples are deliberately ordinary: headings, lists, a quotation, a
figure, a table, a footnote, mathematics, and citations.
Together they show the package at work without wrapping standard document
structures in a second API.
\end{articleabstract}
